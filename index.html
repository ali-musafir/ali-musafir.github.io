🤖 What Is track1?

track1 is a ready-to-use package for experimenting with robots that understand plain language. It is built for the RoboWorld Competition Track 1, a challenge focused on embodied navigation. In simple terms, the software lets you give directions like "move to the blue door" and watch how a virtual robot plans its route.

The toolkit includes world-modeling tools, vision-language understanding, and a JEPA-based approach for predicting movement. These features help you see how modern embodied AI systems make decisions. Everything is designed to run on a normal Windows computer, so you can learn and compete without a technical background.

    💡 Tips for a Better Experience

    Small habits make the toolkit easier to enjoy:

    • Use short instructions. "Go to the door" usually works better than a long sentence with many details.
    • Change one thing at a time. If you change the scene and the instruction together, it becomes hard to tell what caused the difference.
    • Replay before you judge. A single run can be misleading, so watch two or three replays of the same scene.
    • Take notes in a simple text file. Writing down what you typed and what happened helps you spot patterns.
    • Keep the toolkit in one folder. Staying organised makes it easy to return to later.
    • Give yourself time. Embodied AI topics feel unfamiliar at first, and that is completely normal.

    ❓ Common Questions

    Do I need to know how to code? No. track1 is meant to be explored by clicking and typing. If you can use a web browser, you can use this toolkit.

    Is track1 free? Yes. It is the official toolkit of the RoboWorld Competition Track 1 and is shared openly for learning and practice.

    What does "language-conditioned" mean? It simply means the robot is guided by words. You say what you want, and the software translates that into movement.

    What is a world model? A world model is the robot's rough picture of what might happen next. It helps the robot choose a sensible step rather than guessing.

    Do I need to join the competition? No. You can use track1 purely for practice and curiosity. Many people use it simply to understand the ideas.

    Where do I start if I feel lost? Visit the download link, save the toolkit, then open the very first sample scene and try one short instruction. One small step is enough.
